    NUTRITIONAL PROFILE

    HERO INGREDIENTS THAT DELIVER

    BIOACTIVE COLLAGEN PEPTIDES

    Collagen is the primary protein in the body, vital for strength and elasticity in tissues like skin, bones, tendons, and ligaments. As we age, natural collagen levels decrease, accelerated by factors such as UV exposure, pollution, and hormonal changes, leading to visible signs of aging and weakened mobility.

    Our formulation features two distinct collagen peptides, Verisol® and Fortigel®, renowned for their efficacy in addressing both skin and body health.

    These peptides are rich in amino acids like glycine and proline, crucial for tissue health and integrity. Collagen supplementation appeals to a diverse range of individuals, including those seeking benefits for skin, hair, nails, gut, and joint health, athletes, individuals addressing aging concerns, pursuing overall well-being, joint support, or post-surgery recovery. Learn more about this ingredient here.
    HYALURONIC ACID

    Hyaluronic acid (HA) is a natural substance found in the body, especially in the eyes, joints, and skin. It retains water to keep tissues lubricated and moist. With age, levels decrease, leading to joint pain and dry skin. As a supplement HA (sodium hyaluronate), it may enhance skin hydration and elasticity, and support joint health by providing lubrication and cushioning. Produced via fermentation, it yields a pure, low molecular weight product for optimal absorption and effectiveness. Read more about HA here.
    VITAMIN C

    Vitamin C, aka Ascorbic Acid, is considered essential for tissue growth, repair, and various bodily functions, including collagen formation, iron absorption, and immune system support. It is recognised for its role as a potent antioxidant, helping to protect against free radicals and environmental pollutants. Since the body cannot produce Vitamin C, it must be obtained through diet or supplements. Our formulation provides Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid) 100% of the recommended daily intake.

    Collagen and Vitamin C have been shown to have a synergistic relationship, benefiting skin health, hair, nails, joint health, athletic performance, and overall well-being. Learn more about this ingredient here.
